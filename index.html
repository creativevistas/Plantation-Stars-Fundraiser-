ABOUT THEO

Theodore Vista is a dedicated 12U AAA/Majors travel baseball player and versatile utility athlete who loves every part of the game. A former Plantation Stars Red 12U player, Theo has built his skills through competitive tournaments and year-round training with Track Flex, Coach Medina of Infield Development, and Reyes Program. His hard work, determination, and love for baseball keep him striving to improve every day. His biggest dream is to play in Cooperstown in 2027, a special experience designed for 12U players. Your support can help make this age-limited opportunity possible and give Theo memories that will last a lifetime. Thank you for being part of his journey!
